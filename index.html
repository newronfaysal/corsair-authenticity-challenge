HOW TO PLAY

  1. Press PLAY GAME. Boxes of CORSAIR Vengeance RGB DDR5 fall from the top.
  2. Catch the boxes that have the Smart Warranty sticker — they are the real deal.
  3. Avoid boxes without the sticker. Catching one costs points and a life.
  4. Catch several official boxes in a row for a combo multiplier (up to x5).
  5. At the halfway mark a STICKER STORM hits: catch as many official boxes as you can for double points.
  6. The round ends after 60 seconds or when you run out of lives.
